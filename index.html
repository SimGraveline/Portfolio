About Me

I'm Sim, a systems game designer and 2D level designer based in St-Hubert, Quebec. I've spent over 25 years in game development, starting as a game tester in 1999 and going on to design missions for Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood and levels for Panzer Paladin and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Shredder's Revenge, leading design teams along the way.

I co-founded Grave Danger Games and Isart Digital Montreal, where I built the game design program and taught design and prototyping. Today I'm a solo developer at Grave Games, making my own games, like DICEBITS. I graduated in programming at Ahuntsic College in 2026.

I'm looking for a remote role, ideally in mobile or 2D indie games, with human-scale teams that ship.

I'm creative and organized, and I enjoy juggling project priorities, team motivation and company stakes, all while making the best games possible.
